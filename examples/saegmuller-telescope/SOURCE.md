# Source: Equatorial telescope mounting (US Patent 395,002)

- **Title:** Equatorial Mounting for Telescopes, US Patent No. 395,002, drawing sheet 1 (Figs. 1-3)
- **Inventor:** George N. Saegmuller
- **Year:** patented December 25, 1888
- **Original:** https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/40/d1/65/6969a24289e21f/US395002-drawings-page-1.png
  (patent page: https://patents.google.com/patent/US395002)
- **License:** public domain (US patent published before 1929; https://www.uspto.gov/terms-use-uspto-websites)
- **Downloaded:** 2026-11-26, file kept byte for byte as `sketch.png` (2320 x 3408 px)

**What it demonstrates:** turning a partial, part-sectioned 19th-century elevation
into a 45-part assembly: a refractor (tube, objective cell and lens, dew shield,
rack-and-pinion focuser with drawtube and eyepiece, finder on two brackets) held
by two clamped bands in a cradle on a German equatorial mount (polar and
declination housings and shafts, worm wheels and worms with slow-motion
handles, graduated circles, counterweights) on a latitude pivot, a turned pier
and a three-legged base with leveling screws. It shows revolved profiles,
cylinders and holes along X/Y/Z, polar patterns (gear teeth, circle notches,
legs), linear patterns (worm threads), a wedge extrusion, and placements tilted
to the polar axis, the declination axis and the tube; the parts the sheet cuts
off are filled in and recorded as assumptions.
